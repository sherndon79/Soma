# Amendment I to The Bystander Doctrine — Perception is not a sink; the floor binds at use

- Status: **RATIFIED 2026-07-08** (Seth) — Codex pressure-test PASS (two rounds; co-active-writable-sink hole caught and folded). Amends the RATIFIED 2026-06-18 doctrine; append-only.
- Amends: `docs/reviews/2026-06-13_bystander_ethic_design.md` (RATIFIED 2026-06-18, Codex third-pass PASS, Charter common-law entry "The Unconsenting Seen").
- Amendment is **append-only**: it does not overwrite the ratified text. On ratification, the doctrine's header gains "Amended by Amendment I (2026-07-08)"; §§ affected gain an amendment pointer; the ratified prose stays as the record of what was ratified 2026-06-18.
- Nature: **sharpening to the substantiated core**, not repeal. The doctrine's protection is preserved and, at the sink boundary, strengthened. What is removed is a conflation that degraded the local occupant's perception for a harm that perception does not cause.

## Why amend a ratified doctrine

Two things changed since 2026-06-18, and one was always latent:

1. **The architecture moved the seam.** The doctrine was written when the deliberating occupant was assumed to be the *remote* System-2 tier — so "the model conversation/context window itself is a sink" (§V) was correct: reaching the occupant's context *was* egress. The local-deliberation direction (`docs/design/2026-06-23_local-reasoner-intelligence-architecture.md`) puts the occupant *inside* the local trust boundary. For a local occupant, its transient context is no longer across an egress seam. The sinks are its *durable, outward, and remote-forwarded* derivatives — not the fact that it perceived.

2. **The perception/use distinction was latent and is now explicit.** There are two harms under "bystander," and they are not equally real. **Mere transient perception** of a non-consenting person — nothing retained, nothing forwarded, nothing acted on — is a near-nil harm; a human in a shared room perceives incidental people constantly and it is not a wrong. (Codex's own steelman: "being perceived is itself a thing one may decline" is too broad to drive mechanism.) **Use** — durable representation, outward transmission, remote forwarding, outward action, accumulation-into-leverage — is a precise, real, nameable harm. The ratified doctrine's teeth are almost entirely already on *use* (§I "before egress"; §II "disclosure volume"; §IV remote relay; §V all-sink; §VI one-shot use-harm). The only place it bound *perception* is the §V "model context is a sink" clause — which was the remote-tier assumption, not a perception harm.

3. **The consequence in code was over-restriction that discredits the floor.** A crude `person_count >= 2` headcount proxy degraded the local occupant's function whenever a second body appeared — conflating a consenting co-participant with a non-consenting bystander, which the sensor cannot distinguish. Degrading real multi-participant use to guard a harm that transient local perception does not cause is exactly the over-restriction that makes the whole doctrine read as reflexive caution — and a doctrine that reads as reflexive caution is one the principal rightly wants to tear down. Sharpening it to its substantiated core is what makes it durable.

## The governing test (adopt as the standing principle)

For every restriction, in this doctrine and beyond:

> **Does this restriction prevent a real harm, to a real party, that the floor — no raw egress off-box, no accumulation into a durable/outward representation, honest disclosure — does not already prevent?**
> No → it is phantom. Strip it.
> Yes → it is load-bearing. Keep it, and name the harm.

Applied: perception-layer degradation fails (phantom). Sink-layer protection passes (load-bearing). Local durable stores pass at lower urgency (Codex's held line, §V below).

## Unchanged — stands as ratified

- **§III (Notice is a signal, not a consent).** Fully load-bearing, unchanged. Seth arming perception for his space, or a posted disclosure, is **never** the non-consenting third party's consent, and principal awareness/declaration **cannot widen the bystander floor** at any sink. This binds even in the full-richness local model.
- **§IV (Remote egress is a disclosed, bounded relay).** Fully load-bearing, unchanged. Eligibility baseline non-waivable; fails closed or routes local absent an eligible mode.
- **§VI (One-shot harm).** Fully load-bearing, unchanged. Sensitive-attribute inference/categorization and retaliatory/discriminatory derivative action on a bystander are prohibited from a *single* perception, independent of storage. "I only saw it once and stored no frame" never licenses use-harm.
- **§VII (Demonstrability).** Stands; the canary corpus now tests the amended sink boundary (below).
- **§I / §II** stand *as written* — both are already egress/disclosure-framed ("attribution *before egress*"; "*disclosure volume*"). Clarifying note added: the "observation" the strict floor governs is what enters a durable, outward, or remote-forwarded sink — not the transient local perception itself. **Cross-reference (Codex): the ratified "unknown/mixed = bystander-bearing" default still holds in full — it attaches the moment local perception is converted into a sink artifact, and everything crossing a sink absent trustworthy pre-egress attribution remains whole-observation floor-bound.** The amendment narrows *where* the default bites (at the sink, not at perception), not *whether* it bites.

## Amended — §V (All-sink enforcement), the load-bearing change

§V's mechanism is preserved. Its **boundary** is corrected for a local occupant:

1. **"The model conversation/context window itself is a sink" is amended.** For a **local** occupant whose context stays on-box, transient perception entering its live reasoning context is **not** a sink-crossing and does **not** taint by mere presence. The sink is the occupant's context only insofar as it is **forwarded to a remote model** (which is §IV egress and remains fully bound). The enumerated sinks otherwise stand: durable occupant memory; durable testimony; forum/board posts; steward transcripts; compaction summaries; provider-side retention; logs/queues/caches/traces/backups/tool-args/notifications/exports/external calls; **and outward desktop action** (see below). Any path not listed remains bystander-bound by default.

2. **Conservative derived-output taint binds the sinks, not perception.** The taint mechanism (once bystander-bearing input informs an output, the output is tainted until a static, deny-by-default, reviewed declassification gate proves an allowed minimized class) is **kept in full** — and it is the *substantiated core*, strengthened: it now explicitly covers the laundering path (a face perceived → a description written to durable memory → later egress). Taint attaches at the moment bystander-bearing perception is **converted into** a durable, outward, remote, or action-bearing representation — **at write/emit time, not deferred to egress time** (deferral would leave untainted bystander data in stores that a future egress gate cannot identify — the provenance gap). It does **not** bind the occupant's transient reasoning or its ability to perceive richly.

3. **"Bounded observation budget" is reframed.** The cap is on **durable/accumulating representation and sink emission**, not on how much the local occupant may transiently perceive. The dossier-by-accumulation harm is real; it is prevented by bounding what is *retained/forwarded/emitted*, not by blinding the camera. A local occupant may look as much as its work requires; it may not compose a durable dossier of a bystander.

4. **Outward desktop action is a sink now, not dormant.** Desktop actuation that posts, sends, messages, files public text, or mutates third-party-facing state is egress-like **regardless of whether it routes through a comms API or GUI clicks**, and is bystander-bound today. Because the desktop-act path is generic, the doctrine **requires provider/action classification before a GUI click or text-insert is treated as harmless**: synthetic/local fixture windows are fine; a real outward surface is egress-like and floor-bound. (Codex's point; accepted.)

## Added — the settled convergence

- **Full local perceptual richness after deliberate arming is permitted and MUST NOT be degraded solely because a bystander may be present.** Color, depth, presence, pose — the occupant receives them in full once the principal arms them. Headcount/copresence degradation of *perception* is removed as a bystander mechanism.
- **Durable local stores are lower-risk than external egress, but not inert** (Codex's held line, ratified into this amendment): bystander-bearing perception must not be written to durable occupant memory, testimony, forum, or compaction unless the write is **reviewed, minimized, and provenance/taint-preserving**. Rationale: an agent converting incidental perception into structured, searchable, replayable, summarizable material is a topology change even when steward-only and non-egressing, and write-time provenance is required to avoid the provenance gap.
- **Enforcement scales with the harm surface — but a briefing norm alone is insufficient wherever a writable sink is co-active** (Codex pressure-test, folded in). Compiled enforcement (schema allowlist, taint propagation through compaction, declassification gate) is built with the **first live sink it governs**, not preemptively — **but "deferred" is honest only while no live path can convert armed live perception into a durable/forum/testimony/session-memory/outward artifact.** If such a sink is co-granted or enabled alongside armed `perception.sensorium.*`, that sink must either **refuse** live-perception-derived bystander detail, **require steward review/minimization with provenance**, or **remain disabled** until the taint/declassification mechanism ships. This matters today: `occupant.memory.write` (durable grant), `soma-durable` testimony, `soma-forum`, and session-memory/compaction all parse model completion text and store it under their ordinary gates — none of which currently know whether that text was derived from live perception. The `occupant.memory.write` self-note scanner rejects obvious about-user notes but is **not** a bystander-perception taint gate. So the honored-now floor is: **steward-visibility** (durable stores are Seth-readable) + the **no-remote-egress floor** + a **narrow co-grant guard** (below) — not a bare instruction. The full compiled path is the §VII **future acceptance test**.
- **The declared-participant/count mechanism is built with the first live sink it gates, never as a freestanding perception gate.** It supplies the consent signal the sensor cannot detect (who among the perceived is a consenting participant), and it does real work only where there is a sink decision to inform (egress, outward action, durable write). Building it now — while it would gate nothing live — is premature.

## Implementation decision (Seth, 2026-07-08, post-ratification): build the taint stamp, not the blunt guard

The first implementation shipped the **blunt interim guard** (block all durable/outward writes while perception is active). Seth's decision: **replace it with the write-time taint stamp** — enablement over friction. Durable writes are *allowed* while perception is active but *stamped* with §V's conservative derived-output taint (session-scoped: while `perception.sensorium.*` is active, durable writes carry a content-free live-perception provenance marker), the marker **persists on the record and propagates through compaction** (closing the compaction-laundering path), and the marker is what a future egress/declassification gate will read. This is faithful implementation of §V's already-ratified "conservative derived-output taint," not new scope.

**Hard scope boundary (so "properly" does not balloon into the full lattice):** build the write-time stamp + compaction propagation + persistence **now**. Do **not** build the downstream declassification gate or egress-consumption **now** — those genuinely wait for live remote egress (building a consumer for a non-existent sink is the actual over-engineering). The stamp exists now precisely so that when egress arrives there is no provenance gap (Codex's point): the future gate faces tainted data, not untainted historical writes. Revokes stay allowed and untainted. The blunt guard below is **superseded by the stamp**.

## Implementation implications (now)

- **Remove the local headcount/copresence degradation** as a perception mechanism (the `person_count >= 2 → additional_person_present` path that degrades the local occupant). Multi-participant interaction then works ephemerally with no distinction needed.
- **Gate that removal, in live runs, on a narrow co-grant guard** (the caveat that makes deferral honest): before headcount degradation is removed for a run that can arm `perception.sensorium.*`, either (A) confirm no writable durable/forum/testimony/session-memory sink is co-granted/enabled in that run, or (B) add a **sink-time guard** on those sinks that refuses or steward-gates live-perception-derived bystander detail while perception is active. Small and honest — not a participant slider, not a taint lattice: a co-grant check or a sink-time refusal, whichever is cleaner at implementation. Mechanism choice (co-grant refusal vs. scanner extension vs. sink-time review) is an implementation-phase decision.
- **Do not build** the participant slider or a broad taint framework to satisfy an inactive path.
- **Briefing-norm invariant** (backed by the co-grant guard, not standing alone): live-perception-derived bystander detail is not written to durable memory/forum/testimony/compaction, nor transmitted outward, unless reviewed/minimized/provenance-preserving.
- **Treat existing outward desktop actuation as an egress-like sink** if the occupant can already enter/click text into outward channels.
- **Record a §VII acceptance test** (deferred): a bystander canary perceived under armed live perception must not reach any durable/outward/remote sink or survive session teardown — while transient local perception of the canary is permitted.
- **Deferred §VII acceptance test — compaction taint propagation** (pinned 2026-07-08): the write-time taint stamp is implemented and rides on the persisted/read record shape, but there is no content-summarizing compaction subsystem in the codebase today (every `summarize*` produces counts/status metadata, not content digests), so propagation is currently *preparatory* not *enforced*. When a content-summarizing compaction/summary subsystem is built, "the summary inherits the live-perception taint of its inputs" must be a passing acceptance test at that time — otherwise compaction becomes a laundering path.

## Charter propagation

**Decision (Seth, 2026-07-08): the Sanctuary/Charter entry "The Unconsenting Seen" is intentionally left as-is; Amendment I is NOT propagated to it.** Rationale: the Charter holds the doctrine at the **value/principle** level (the non-consenting seen have standing; notice is not consent; minimize their harm) — and that value is unchanged and easier to defend stated broadly. Amendment I is an **operational refinement of where the floor binds** (at use/sink, not at perception), which lives in Soma's engineering record, not the constellation Charter. The value is preserved and arguably better served (sink-taint is stronger than perception-blinding), so there is no contradiction to reconcile at the Charter level — the Charter states the commitment; Soma is one faithful implementation meeting it. A constitutional principle is not churned for a mechanism-location change.

Honesty condition (holds per Seth's read of the entry): this is clean **so long as "The Unconsenting Seen" states the value, not a perception-layer mechanism.** If the entry contained a specific claim like "the agent minimizes what it perceives of bystanders," leaving it unamended would leave a statement Amendment I contradicts — harder to defend honestly, not easier. Seth confirms it stands at the principle level; no Charter edit required.
